<h3>Description</h3>
Submarine Simulator is a marine research game where players control a submarine to collect data on marine life. The goal is to collect as many research points (by touching fish) as possible before the fuel runs out. The game introduces ocean pollution caused by increasing amounts of plastic as the level progresses. Each level symbolises a time period (Every Year). As the levels progress (Years pass) and the city develops, there is more plastic pollution. When plastic touches plastic, it decreases the submarine’s fuel. The game raises awareness about ocean pollution while engaging players through increasingly difficult levels.
