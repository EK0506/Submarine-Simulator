Submarine Simulator is a marine research game where players control a submarine to collect data on marine life. The goal is to collect as many research points (by touching fish) as possible before the fuel runs out. The game introduces the ocean pollution issue by the increasing plastic as level goes on. Each level symbolises a time period (Every Year). As the levels progress (Year passes), due to the development of the city, there are more plastic pollution. When plastic touches plastic it decreases the submarine’s fuel. The game raises awareness about ocean pollution while engaging players through increasingly difficult levels.
The project has been discontinued as the school assessment has been completed
